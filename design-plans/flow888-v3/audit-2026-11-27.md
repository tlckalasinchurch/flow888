### Inspector and lint items that conflict with DESIGN.md

DESIGN.md is the owner-approved contract and wins over the taste skill's marketing-page house
rules. These items are defended, not fixed:

- Uppercase 12px tracked labels (`micro-label`): DESIGN.md typography `label` (700, 12px, 0.06em,
  uppercase) for channel numbers, tags and pills.
- 1px borders (`hairline`, `outlined-box`): DESIGN.md "Depth is the navy ladder plus 1px borders".
- Inset shadows (`shadow`, `button-fx`): DESIGN.md Shadow Vocabulary, state bars and selection
  rings are inset marks that mean state.
- `#A78BFA` (`palette`): DESIGN.md Signal Violet, the FX mix identity.
- Meter hard-stop gradient: green/amber/red zones, not a decorative blend.
- `padStart(2,'0')`: channel numbers 01-07 as printed on the mixer.

Items not covered by DESIGN.md and still open: `contrast` ("MUTE" 3.5:1) and `tiny-text`
(53% of text under 13px).

## Design language
- Audited surface: FLOW 888 v3 console, `/v3/` (`flow8/web_ui/v3/index.html`, `v3.css`, `v3.js`), all 8 pages.
- Design sources: `DESIGN.md` (Night Console, owner-approved 2026-11-26); `design-plans/flow888-v3/README.md` (v3 decisions).
- Documented decisions: Signal Teal = selection and focus (DESIGN.md "Primary"; "Selection is a teal border or fill"); Amber = caution and degraded state; Emerald = MON identity; Violet = FX identity; Colour-Is-Meaning Rule; Unknown-Reads-Unknown Rule; eight strips fit without shrinking targets.
- Governing owners and consumers: tokens in `v3.css:6-15` (`--sel`, `--amber`, `--mix-mon`, `--mix-fx`), consumed by every v3 page.
- Explicit exceptions: None documented.

## Question for the owner (not a finding: the evidence supports two corrections)
DESIGN.md asks for a 3-letter provenance badge (UNS / CMD / OBS). v3 shows words (LIVE / SENT /
UNKNOWN, v3 README). On My Mon at 1180 the word UNKNOWN pushes the channel name to "Channel …".
Either v3 goes back to 3 letters, or DESIGN.md is updated to the v3 words and the strip head
gets room for them. This is the owner's choice.
